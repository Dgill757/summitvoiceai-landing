# SummitVoiceAI Design Direction

## Three directions considered

### Theme Name: Signal & Steel
**Very Brief Intro:** A premium industrial-information system where revenue signals move visibly through a dark field of steel-blue surfaces. It feels decisive, technical, and tailored to serious roofing operators rather than software tourists.

**Probability:** 0.07

### Theme Name: Field Ledger
**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, editorial financial-report direction pairing blueprint surfaces with close-cropped roofing photography and hard data. It emphasizes accountability and the economics hidden inside an owner’s operating system.

**Probability:** 0.04

### Theme Name: Night Shift Dispatch
**Very Brief Intro:** A high-contrast nocturnal operations center inspired by after-hours calls, field radios, and live dispatch boards. It makes missed calls and slow response feel immediate without falling into cyberpunk tropes.

**Probability:** 0.09

## Chosen direction: Signal & Steel

### Design Movement
Industrial information design meets premium technical editorial. The page behaves like a clear, living operating system: exceptionally legible data, deliberate moments of drama, and disciplined hierarchy.

### Core Principles
1. Treat every section as evidence in a revenue-recovery argument, never as decorative content.
2. Use a flowing visual path to show an opportunity moving from signal to system to booked appointment.
3. Maintain strong contrast, generous breathing space, and a data-forward hierarchy for owner-level scanning.
4. Use restrained motion only to reveal movement, response time, and recovery—not to decorate.

### Color Philosophy
The foundation is deep slate-navy, like a professional operations room at night. Electric cyan represents a live opportunity in motion, while disciplined cobalt distinguishes system infrastructure and muted warm sandstone signals a risk or a leak. Purple is excluded from the primary experience so the brand feels grounded in roofing operations rather than generic AI marketing.

### Layout Paradigm
The experience follows a single diagonal “revenue signal” path that cuts through an asymmetric editorial composition. Content blocks alternate between a compact left rail of context and an expanded right field of visual evidence, rather than repeating generic centered card grids.

### Signature Elements
1. A thin cyan signal line that travels through key sections, terminating at a booked appointment.
2. Technical corner notches, field-label eyebrow copy, and compact measurement-style annotations.
3. Glassy dark interface panels on a textured navy background, with real-feeling pipeline states and no sci-fi decoration.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions explain cause and effect. Calculator values update immediately; timeline and loop nodes make the next operational step clear; controls provide tactile feedback while keeping the audit CTA continuously obvious.

### Animation
Use short (160–280ms) transform-and-opacity transitions, staggered content entry, and a subtle cyan flow pulse. The Revenue Recovery Loop activates in sequence as it enters the viewport. Number changes use controlled count transitions. All non-essential movement disables under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
**Barlow Condensed** is the assertive display face for compact, decisive claims and data labels. **DM Sans** is the body face for plainspoken clarity and readable numeric forms. Headlines use tight all-caps or sentence case with high contrast; body copy is short, comfortable, and never ornamental.

### Brand Essence
**SummitVoiceAI installs the revenue-recovery infrastructure that helps established roofing companies stop wasting opportunities they already paid to create.**

**Personality:** exacting, grounded, accountable.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are decisive, economically concrete, and rooted in a roofer’s real operating day. CTAs are direct but not pushy; microcopy reduces ambiguity rather than performing excitement.

Example: “Someone called at 8:37 PM. What happened next?”

Example: “See the highest-priority leaks before you buy another lead.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a bold, text-free geometric summit mark: an ascending roofline that becomes a single continuous signal path, with a small cyan signal point at the peak. The header pairs the mark with a condensed custom wordmark treatment rather than a default font lock-up.

### Signature Brand Color
**Signal Cyan — #00D4FF.**

## Style Decisions

- The hero uses a low-key, night-time roof operations visual so its overlay text remains white and highly legible.
- Every visual component must answer: “Does this choice reinforce or dilute our design philosophy?”
- The calculator is the visual and conversion centerpiece: a two-column diagnostic board on desktop and an intentional vertical progression on mobile.
